Privacy policy.

How we handle personal information when you visit our website or contact our studio.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

About this policy

Animeghaa Production House (we, us or our) operates animeghaa.com.au and provides animation and creative services. This policy explains our handling of personal information connected with this website and enquiries about our services.

Information we collect

When you contact us by email or phone, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, studio or company name, project brief, references, timeline and any other information you choose to provide. If you engage us, we may also receive information needed to prepare quotes, manage the project and maintain business records.

Please provide only information relevant to your enquiry. Avoid sending sensitive personal information or identifiable patient information in project materials unless we have agreed an appropriate way to handle it.

How we collect and use information

We collect enquiry information directly from you and, where relevant, from an authorised representative. We use it to respond to enquiries, discuss briefs, prepare quotes, communicate about and deliver requested services, maintain related records and meet applicable legal obligations. If you do not provide contact details or essential project information, we may be unable to respond or provide a quote.

Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list. We do not sell your personal information.

Website technology and external services

This website does not currently include Google Analytics, advertising pixels, an enquiry form or user accounts. Portfolio videos are served directly as website files. We have not added analytics or advertising cookies to the site.

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting and network providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details and requested pages to deliver and secure the website. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Clicking our email link opens Gmail with a draft enquiry template. The draft is not sent to us until you send it. Google may process information when you use Gmail under Google’s privacy policy. Other external websites have their own privacy practices.

Sharing and overseas processing

Information may be handled by providers used for website hosting, email, file storage and business administration, or by people assisting with your requested project where necessary. We may also disclose information where required or authorised by law. We limit sharing to what is reasonably needed for those purposes.

Our website hosting and Gmail services involve providers based in the United States. These providers may store or process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Their privacy policies explain their international processing practices. Contact us if you need information about how a particular enquiry or project is handled.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information we hold against loss, misuse and unauthorised access or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for enquiries, projects, business records and applicable legal obligations, then take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it when it is no longer required.

Access, correction and deletion requests

You can contact us to request access to or correction of personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete information that is no longer needed. We may need to verify your identity. We will consider your request and explain any limitations, including records we need to retain for legal or legitimate business purposes.

Privacy questions and complaints

Contact us using the details below and describe your concern. We will review it, contact you if we need more information and aim to respond within 30 days. If more time is needed, we will let you know. If you remain dissatisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, where the matter falls within its jurisdiction.
